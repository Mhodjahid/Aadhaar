About AADHAAR

An Association for Development, Harmony and Action Research — a nationally registered not-for-profit organization working to build strong models of community engagement across India since 2011.

Our Story

From a shared vision to a national movement

AADHAAR — An Association for Development, Harmony and Action Research — came into formal existence on Jan 29th, 2011 as a nationally registered not-for-profit organization. Realizing that only strong models of community engagement could bring necessary changes at the community level and can be point of references for policy advocacy, a group of like-minded, development professionals got together and after much discussion and deliberations, AADHAAR came into being.

We believe that durable change begins at the grassroots. Our approach combines direct community engagement with rigorous action research, so that the models we build on the ground can inform and shape policy at a larger scale.

  • Community engagement as the foundation of all our work.
  • Action research that turns field learnings into policy advocacy.
  • Partnership across public, private and civil society sectors.

What Drives Us

Our vision, mission & values

Our Vision

A society where strong, self-reliant communities drive their own development and where grassroots models of engagement become the foundation for inclusive policy.

Our Mission

To build strong models of community engagement that bring necessary change at the community level and serve as points of reference for policy advocacy.

Our Values

Development, harmony and action research — carried with integrity, inclusiveness and a deep respect for the communities we serve.
