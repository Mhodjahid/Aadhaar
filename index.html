About AADHAAR

Strong models of community engagement bring necessary change

AADHAAR — An Association for Development, Harmony and Action Research — came into formal existence on Jan 29th, 2011 as a nationally registered not-for-profit organization. Realizing that only strong models of community engagement could bring necessary changes at the community level and can be point of references for policy advocacy, a group of like-minded, development professionals got together and after much discussion and deliberations, AADHAAR came into being.

  • Nationally registered not-for-profit organization since 2011.
  • Strong community engagement models as references for policy advocacy.
  • A collective of like-minded development professionals driving real impact.

What We Do

Thematic areas of work

Our work spans interconnected themes that together build resilient, self-reliant communities.

01

Education

Strengthening school education, online learning access and vocational education to build employability among India's youth.

02

Skill Development

Equipping young people entering the workforce with vocational and life skills that drive economic growth and social development.

03

Women Empowerment

Digitally empowering women, building self-help groups and enabling leadership at the community level.

04

Community Development

Strong models of community engagement that serve as references for policy advocacy and lasting change.

05

Action Research

Evidence-based research that informs policy and programme design at the grassroots and national level.

06

Policy Advocacy

Turning community-level learnings into advocacy that shapes inclusive public policy.
